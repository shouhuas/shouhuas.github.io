How to Build a Simple Household Budget

A household budget gives your income a plan. Start with real spending records, choose realistic limits and review the plan regularly.

List take-home income, essential bills, flexible spending, debt payments and savings. Use recent statements rather than memory.

Understand the basics

A common framework separates needs, wants and future goals. Percentages are only starting points; local costs and circumstances differ.

Practical steps

Track one month of spending, set manageable limits and review the plan monthly.

Keep it practical: Choose one manageable action, test it in your circumstances and review the result.

What to keep in mind

Do not use new debt to make a budget appear balanced.
