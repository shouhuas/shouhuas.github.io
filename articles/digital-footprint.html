Your Digital Footprint, Explained

Understand the information created when you browse, post, search, shop and use connected devices.

Some information is actively shared, such as public posts. Other information is generated passively, such as device identifiers and interaction logs.

Understand the basics

A digital footprint can be spread across accounts, apps, websites and backups.
