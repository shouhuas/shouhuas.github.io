A Practical Guide to Critical Thinking

Use simple questions to evaluate claims and evidence before accepting or sharing information.

Separate a claim from the evidence offered for it. A confident tone or large number of shares does not prove it true.

A quick check includes identifying the source, inspecting evidence and seeking independent confirmation.
